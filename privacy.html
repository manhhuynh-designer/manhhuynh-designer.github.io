Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

PromptAgent Flow Bridge

PromptAgent Flow Bridge is a productivity tool designed to bridge Google Flow and Google Gemini. Your privacy is critically important to us.

1. Information Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, browser history, or generated images to any third-party servers. All data processing and clipboard transfers happen completely locally on your machine within your browser.

2. Required Permissions

The extension requests access to tabs, activeTab, and specific host permissions (labs.google and gemini.google.com) strictly for the purpose of facilitating the transfer of generated images between these two specific Google services. We do not track your activity on these sites or any other websites.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension interacts directly with Google Flow and Google Gemini. Usage of those services is governed by Google's respective Privacy Policies and Terms of Service.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please reach out to us at contact@manhhuynh.work.
